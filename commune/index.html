The place

A hidden gem on the busiest strip

Commune is a small room under a black awning at 359 Brunswick St, the kind of place a strip like Fitzroy's hides in plain sight. It is owner run, and it reads that way: reviewers keep naming the welcome before they name the food, and the room stays cosy and unpretentious, warm timber, warm light, the window seat looking straight onto the street.

The kitchen calls itself modern Turkish brunch and means it. A simple, quality-driven menu with seasonal dishes and beautiful Turkish touches, fresh pastries baked in house, and specialty coffee poured in collaboration with Spectrum Coffee. The letterboard keeps it honest: black, white, batch brew, pour over. Nothing that needs explaining.
