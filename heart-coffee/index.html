The corner

Painted on, not printed.

There is no vinyl logo at Heart Coffee. The name is painted by hand, twice: once in big white strokes across the shop window, and once in ink on the sign that hangs over the footpath. Behind the glass the room is bright, airy and minimalist, white walls and pale timber, a little modern and a little retro, with indoor plants in every corner.

The coffee is built on Dukes Coffee Roasters blends, black or white, with a heart poured into the flat whites more often than not. The kitchen keeps it simple and does it properly: toasted sandwiches stacked the right way, house-baked treats on the counter, and the cinnamon scrolls most people are really here for.

The signature

The cinnamon scroll.

Richly glazed, pillow-soft and served warm. It is the first thing most reviews mention, and one calls it the best in Melbourne outright. The scroll changes with the seasons, takes savoury turns, and the ham and cheese version has a following of its own.
