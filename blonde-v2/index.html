Blonde Food and Drink sits at 443 Brunswick Street, in the middle of the Fitzroy strip. The kitchen runs one printed all day menu, and it is genuinely big, from toast and granola through to a 48 hour braised lamb hash bowl. Espresso, batch and matcha at the counter, milk drinks finished with real latte art.
