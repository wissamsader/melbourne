The cook

Hello habibi. Come in hungry.

The cabinet at Little Bridge is filled on site every morning by the cook the reviews keep naming. Hummus and labneh in the painted bowls, za'atar mana'ish warm from the press, falafel fried in small batches, a soup of the day, and a pot of something slow, fasolia with rice on a good day. When the cabinet empties, that is lunch over.

She runs the room the way she runs the pots, personally. Walk in twice and she remembers you, your order and all, and greets you with a hello habibi over the counter, however deep the lunch queue gets. Nothing fancy, nothing styled. Proper Lebanese home cooking, generous plates, and board prices that make you look twice. Regulars call it one of Richmond's best-kept secrets, then keep telling people anyway.
