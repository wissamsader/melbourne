The room on Brunswick Street

A big menu, done properly, at a fair price

Blonde Food and Drink sits at 443 Brunswick Street, in the middle of the Fitzroy strip. The registered name is the long one, but plenty of regulars have shortened it to Blondie, which tells you the kind of place it is. The room is painted a soft sage green, the chairs are black spindle-backs with olive leather seats, and the floor keeps its old patterned tiles.

The kitchen runs one printed all day menu, and it is genuinely big: hotcakes, shakshuka, a 48 hour braised lamb hash, croquettes, a benedict built on a house made hash brown. Reviewers keep landing on the same two points, that there is something for everyone, and that the prices stay reasonable. The coffee is taken as seriously as the food, which in this suburb is saying something.
